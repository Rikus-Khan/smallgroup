The same God who broke Pharaoh's grip on His people will break the beast's. Egypt's plagues ended with God's people walking free. So will these.

True and righteous

Two voices answer the third bowl. The angel of the waters says God is righteous. A voice out of the altar, where the martyrs cried “How long?” (6:9–10), says “Even so.”

They are worthy means they deserve it. The punishment fits the crime exactly. No more, no less.

“… Shall not the Judge of all the earth do right?”

Genesis 18:25

You can put it down

Some of us were badly wronged: by a father, by a man on the street, by the system. Some of us did the wronging. Both kinds of men carry it around.

“Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves, but rather give place unto wrath: for it is written, Vengeance is mine; I will repay, saith the Lord.”

Romans 12:19

If you were wronged: God keeps the books perfectly. You don't have to settle that score. Hand it to Him today, and let it go.

If you did the wronging: the same just God laid your sin on His Son. “There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus” (Romans 8:1). Justice was done, at the cross.

They knew who was doing it: they blasphemed the name of God, which hath power over these plagues. They didn't doubt Him. They hated Him, and wouldn't bend. Pharaoh did the same: “when Pharaoh saw that there was respite, he hardened his heart” (Exodus 8:15).

The door is still open today

Every “no” to God makes the next one easier. That's true of a whole world in Revelation 16, and it's true of one man at a kitchen table. Pain didn't change them. Hitting bottom doesn't save anybody by itself either. Only grace does.

“The Lord is not slack concerning his promise, as some men count slackness; but is longsuffering to us-ward, not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.”

2 Peter 3:9

“But exhort one another daily, while it is called To day; lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin.”

Hebrews 3:13

That is what this group is for: daily, while it is called To day. If there's something you keep saying no to God about, the habit, the person, the confession you keep putting off, say yes to Him today, while it's still easy to hear.

Part three

Gathered to Armageddon

Verses 12–16

A river dries up, lies go out, and in the middle of it all, Jesus speaks.

The great river Euphrates

  1. 1Earth
  2. 2Sea
  3. 3Rivers
  4. 4Sun
  5. 5Beast's seat
  6. 6Euphrates
  7. 7Air

The Euphrates was the edge of the land promised to Abraham (Genesis 15:18), and in John's day the eastern edge of Rome's world. Beyond it lay the empires that always came west to make war.

God dried up waters before, so His people could cross the Red sea and the Jordan. Here He dries a river so His enemies can come, to their own judgment.

Who are the kings of the east? Teachers have named one nation after another over the years, and been wrong every time. John doesn't name them; neither will we.

Spirits like frogs

The dragon is Satan (12:9). The beast and the false prophet are his men (chapter 13). Three liars, a fake of the Father, Son and Spirit, and out of their mouths come lies.

Frogs were unclean under the law, and a plague on Egypt. Loud, slimy, everywhere. And these work miracles. Signs don't prove a voice is from God:

“Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world.”

1 John 4:1

Jesus breaks in

Right in the middle of the armies gathering, Jesus speaks. A thief doesn't call ahead. He has said it before, to a sleepy church at Sardis (3:3), and Paul says it too:

“But ye, brethren, are not in darkness, that that day should overtake you as a thief.”

1 Thessalonians 5:4

Caught asleep

Walking naked

Ashamed, exposed, caught living the old life when He comes. They see his shame.

Watching

Keeping his garments

“He hath clothed me with the garments of salvation, he hath covered me with the robe of righteousness.” Isaiah 61:10

Many of us know what shame feels like: being found out. Jesus covers a man. Then He tells him to keep those clothes on: stay awake, stay clean, keep short accounts with God. Get dressed for Him every morning.

Armageddon: the hill of Megiddo

Armageddon is Hebrew: Har-Megiddo, the hill of Megiddo. The town sat on a mound guarding the pass through the Carmel ridge, above the wide valley of Jezreel. Armies crossing the land met there again and again:

  1. 1Deborah and Barak beat Sisera “by the waters of Megiddo” Judges 5:19
  2. 2Gideon's three hundred routed Midian in the valley of Jezreel Judges 6:33; 7
  3. 3King Saul fell on mount Gilboa 1 Samuel 31
  4. 4Good King Josiah was killed at Megiddo, and the nation mourned 2 Kings 23:29; Zechariah 12:11

The battle that never really happens

Bible teachers differ here, without heat. Many take it as a real gathering of armies in that very valley. Others read Armageddon as a picture, drawn from all those old battles, of the last stand of evil against God. Both agree on the outcome.

Look ahead to chapter 19. The armies gather, and there's no long fight. One verse later, it's over:

“And I saw the beast, and the kings of the earth, and their armies, gathered together to make war against him that sat on the horse, and against his army.”

Revelation 19:19

“And the beast was taken, and with him the false prophet …”

Revelation 19:20

The devil gathers the whole world against God, and it isn't even close. Whatever is lined up against you today is not bigger than that.

The last bowl goes into the air, everywhere at once. And the voice from the throne says one word in Greek, gegonen: it has happened, it is done. Three times God says “done”:

  1. “It is finished”The cross: the price of sin paidJohn 19:30
  2. “It is done”The last bowl: evil judgedRevelation 16:17
  3. “It is done”All things made newRevelation 21:6

If you belong to Jesus, the first one covers you. That's why the second one doesn't fall on you, and the third one is your home.

Who drinks the cup

Babylon is the great city of the world set against God. Chapters 17 and 18 tell her fall. Teachers differ on whether she is one real city or the whole proud world system, but she is not forgotten: she came in remembrance before God.

What is plain

A talent was a heavy weight, often figured at about 75 pounds. Hail like that, and still men blasphemed God. The last word about them is the saddest in the chapter.

We won't settle every detail of Revelation 16 today. But some things in it are plain, and a man can stand on them:

  • God's judgment is just“true and righteous are thy judgments” (16:7)
  • Jesus is coming“Behold, I come as a thief” (16:15)
  • Evil will be finished“It is done” (16:17)

“And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away.”

Revelation 21:4

Points to remember

  1. Justice“True and righteous are thy judgments” 16:7God's judgments fit exactly. You can hand Him the score you can't settle, and trust the cross with your own.
  2. Repent“They repented not to give him glory” 16:9Every no makes the next one easier. Pain alone doesn't change a heart; only grace does. Say yes today.
  3. Frogs“Three unclean spirits like frogs” 16:13Lies out of the devil's mouth, with miracles to back them. Signs don't prove a voice is from God.
  4. Watch“Blessed is he that watcheth” 16:15Jesus comes like a thief. Stay awake and keep the garments He gave you on.
  5. Armageddon“A place called … Armageddon” 16:16The hill of Megiddo, where armies met for centuries. The whole world gathers against God, and it isn't even close.
  6. Done“It is done” 16:17The cross paid it, the last bowl ends evil, and God makes all things new.

Question 1 of 6

Justice

What do the angels say about God's judgments?

What verses tell us about this?

“Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves, but rather give place unto wrath: for it is written, Vengeance is mine; I will repay, saith the Lord.”

Romans 12:19

Answer

That God is righteous, and His judgments are true and righteous. Those who shed the blood of His people get exactly what they deserve. So we can leave our scores with Him, and trust that at the cross He dealt justly with our own sin.

Question 2 of 6

Repent

What did men do when the plagues fell on them?

What verses tell us about this?

“But exhort one another daily, while it is called To day; lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin.”

Hebrews 3:13

Answer

They blasphemed God and repented not, again and again, knowing it was His hand. Every refusal hardened them more. Pain alone doesn't change a heart. Today the door is still open, so don't put off saying yes to Him.

Question 3 of 6

Frogs

Where do the three unclean spirits come from, and what do they do?

What verses tell us about this?

“Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world.”

1 John 4:1

Answer

Out of the mouths of the dragon, the beast and the false prophet. They are spirits of devils, working miracles, and they go out to gather the kings of the whole world to battle against God. Miracles don't prove a voice is from God: test every spirit.

Question 4 of 6

Watch

What does Jesus say about His coming, and who is blessed?

What verses tell us about this?

“But ye, brethren, are not in darkness, that that day should overtake you as a thief.”

1 Thessalonians 5:4

Answer

He comes as a thief, without warning. Blessed is the man who watches and keeps his garments, so he isn't caught naked and ashamed. Jesus clothes us with His righteousness; we stay awake and live dressed for Him.

Question 5 of 6

Armageddon

Where are the kings of the earth gathered, and how does it end?

What verses tell us about this?

“And the beast was taken, and with him the false prophet that wrought miracles before him, with which he deceived them that had received the mark of the beast, and them that worshipped his image. These both were cast alive into a lake of fire burning with brimstone.”

Revelation 19:20

Answer

At a place called Armageddon, the hill of Megiddo above the valley of Jezreel, where Israel's armies fought for centuries. Teachers differ on literal or picture, but the end is plain: when Christ comes, the beast and the false prophet are taken, and it isn't even close.

Question 6 of 6

Done

What does the voice from the throne say when the seventh vial is poured?

What verses tell us about this?

“When Jesus therefore had received the vinegar, he said, It is finished: and he bowed his head, and gave up the ghost.”

John 19:30

“And he said unto me, It is done. I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end. I will give unto him that is athirst of the fountain of the water of life freely.”

Revelation 21:6

Answer

“It is done.” God's judgment on evil is finished. Jesus said “It is finished” on the cross, paying for the sins of everyone who trusts Him, and God will say “It is done” once more when He makes all things new.
