Part one

Wisdom calls out

Verses 1–11

God's wisdom isn't hiding. She's shouting in the street.

Everywhere you turn

Wisdom stands on the hilltop where everybody can see her, at the crossroads where you make choices, at the city gate where business was done, and right at the front doors.

God doesn't hide what's right and make you hunt for it. He puts it in plain view and raises His voice. The question is whether we'll stop and listen.

Two voices on the same street

Proverbs 7:12 · the strange woman

Now is she without, now in the streets, and lieth in wait at every corner.)

Proverbs 8:3 · wisdom

She crieth at the gates, at the entry of the city, at the coming in at the doors.

Last chapter, one woman waited at the corners in the dark to trap a young man. Now another stands in the open, in daylight, calling out to save him. Both are out where you live. Which voice are you going to follow?

“Unto you, O men, I call”

Look who gets invited: “the sons of man,” “ye simple,” “ye fools.” Not the smart people. Not the ones with their lives together. The guys who've made a mess of things are first on the list.

That's the heart of God. Jesus said the same thing: “Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest” (Matthew 11:28). Nobody is too far gone to be called.

Worth more than all of it

Silver, gold, and rubies were the most valuable things a man could own. Wisdom says, “Take me instead.”

Money can be lost in a night. Plenty of us have done it. Wisdom keeps paying out for the rest of your life, and nobody can take it from you.

Part two

What Wisdom gives

Verses 12–21

Good sense, a clean heart, and riches that last.

Some old words

prudence
good sense; thinking ahead before you act
witty inventions
wise plans. In 1611 “witty” meant wise, not funny.
arrogancy
arrogance; thinking you're above correction
froward
twisted, crooked, bent the wrong way

Verse 13 tells us what the fear of the LORD looks like in real life: you start to hate what He hates. Pride, arrogance, the evil way, a twisted mouth. It's more than being afraid of God. It's honoring Him enough to hate what destroys people.

Seek early, find riches that last

“Seek me early” means seek me first, and seek me hard, like a man up at dawn to get at what matters. Wisdom promises she won't stay hidden from that man.

What this doesn't mean

A fat bank account

This isn't a promise that following God makes you rich. Proverbs are wise sayings about how life usually goes, not guarantees.

What it does mean

Durable riches

Honor, righteousness, a life built right. Wisdom's riches don't burn up, get stolen, or get blown in one bad weekend.

Part three

Before the world began

Verses 22–31

God has never been without His wisdom. Not for one moment.

Who is talking here?

The speaker all through this chapter is Wisdom. The clues tell us how to read her:

  • She's a “she”“She standeth… she crieth” (vv. 2–3)
  • She shouts in the streetat the gates and the doors (v. 3)
  • She lives with prudence“I wisdom dwell with prudence” (v. 12)
  • So this is poetryGod's wisdom pictured as a woman calling out

We talk this way too: “Lady Justice,” “Mother Nature.” Solomon takes something God has, His wisdom, and lets it speak as a person, so we'll listen. And verse 23 says she was there “from everlasting.” God has always been wise. There was never a moment when He wasn't.

Does verse 22 mean Jesus was created?

Jehovah's Witnesses read this as Jesus saying God created Him first. Look at the word.

קָנָה said qanah to get, to have, to own

Proverbs 4:5

Get wisdom, get understanding: forget it not; neither decline from the words of my mouth.

Genesis 14:19

And he blessed him, and said, Blessed be Abram of the most high God, possessor of heaven and earth:

Same word. When you “get” wisdom, you don't create it. God “possessed” His wisdom: He has always had it, as part of who He is.

Christ: the wisdom of God, and the Creator

1 Corinthians 1:24

But unto them which are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ the power of God, and the wisdom of God.

John 1:3

All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made.

Many Christians have seen a picture of Jesus in this chapter, and that's fair as far as it goes: Jesus is God's wisdom in the flesh. Everything true of God's wisdom is true of Him, including “from everlasting.”

But a poem can't overrule plain teaching. John 1 says all things were made by Him. If He made every thing that was made, He isn't one of the made things. “And he is before all things, and by him all things consist” (Colossians 1:17). Jesus isn't God's first creation. He is the Creator.

Already there

Before the depths, the springs, the mountains, or the dust, wisdom was there. That's how Psalm 90:2 talks about God Himself: “Before the mountains were brought forth… even from everlasting to everlasting, thou art God.”

A “compass” here is a circle. God drew the horizon on the face of the waters like a builder marking out his work. “The LORD by wisdom hath founded the earth” (Proverbs 3:19).

“My delights were with the sons of men”

“As one brought up with him” is a hard phrase. The Hebrew word can mean a master workman or a child raised close by. Either way, the picture is joy: wisdom at God's side, delighting in Him and in His work, every day.

God didn't make the world grudgingly. He made it with joy. And look where wisdom's delight landed: on people. On us. That's why the Wisdom of God came to “the sons of men” in person: Jesus, “In whom are hid all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge” (Colossians 2:3).

Part four

Blessed is the man

Verses 32–36

Two doors, life or death. Wisdom is waiting at one of them.

Waiting at the door, daily

Picture a man who shows up at Wisdom's door every morning, before it even opens, and waits. Not once in a while. Daily.

That's what you're doing right now, by being here. Getting into God's Word every day isn't a religious chore. Verse 34 calls that man blessed.

“Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.”

John 14:6

Two doors

Whoso findeth me→findeth lifeand “shall obtain favour of the LORD” (v. 35)
All they that hate me→love deathTurning down wisdom isn't neutral. It wrongs your own soul (v. 36).

Proverbs 8 ends where the gospel begins. Christ “of God is made unto us wisdom” (1 Corinthians 1:30). To find Him is to find life. He calls out to everyone, and the door is open to anyone who comes.

  2. True riches“Wisdom is better than rubies” 8:11Money can be gone in a night. Wisdom pays out for life.
  3. The fear of the LORD“The fear of the LORD is to hate evil” 8:13You start to hate what He hates: pride, arrogance, a twisted mouth.
  4. Seeking“Those that seek me early shall find me” 8:17Seek first and seek hard. The riches are real, and they last.
  5. Who Jesus is“All things were made by him” John 1:3God has always had His wisdom. Christ is that wisdom in person, and the Creator, not a creature.
  6. Life and death“Whoso findeth me findeth life” 8:35Find Christ, the wisdom of God, and you find life.

Question 1 of 6

Wisdom's invitation

Who does Wisdom call to?

What verses tell us about this?

“Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.”

Matthew 11:28

Answer

Everybody. She calls out in public, where everyone can hear, to “the sons of man,” and she names the simple and the fools on purpose. God's invitation isn't only for people who have it together. It's for anyone who'll listen, and that includes us.

Question 2 of 6

True riches

What is worth more than gold?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

Wisdom. Silver, gold, and rubies don't compare. Money can be lost in a night, but wisdom keeps paying out for the rest of your life, and no one can take it from you.

Question 3 of 6

The fear of the LORD

What does the fear of the LORD look like?

What verses tell us about this?

“The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom: and the knowledge of the holy is understanding.”

Proverbs 9:10

Answer

It looks like hating evil: pride, arrogance, the evil way, and a twisted mouth. It's more than being afraid. It's honoring and loving God so much that you start to hate the things He hates, because they destroy people. That's where wisdom starts.

Question 4 of 6

Seeking

What happens to those who seek wisdom?

What verses tell us about this?

“If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.”

James 1:5

Answer

They find it. Wisdom loves those who love her, and she doesn't hide from the man who seeks her first and seeks her hard, showing up daily at her door. And God promises: ask Him for wisdom, and He gives it freely, without putting you down for asking.

Question 5 of 6

Who Jesus is

Does verse 22 mean Jesus was created?

What verses tell us about this?

“But unto them which are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ the power of God, and the wisdom of God.”

1 Corinthians 1:24

“All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made.”

John 1:3

“And he is before all things, and by him all things consist.”

Colossians 1:17

Answer

No. Proverbs 8 is poetry that pictures God's wisdom as a woman calling out. “Possessed” means God had His wisdom, and verse 23 says it was “from everlasting.” God has never been without it. Christ is the wisdom of God in person, but He isn't a creature: all things were made by Him, and He is before all things. He is the Creator.

Question 6 of 6

Life and death

Where is life found?

What verses tell us about this?

“Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.”

John 14:6

“But of him are ye in Christ Jesus, who of God is made unto us wisdom, and righteousness, and sanctification, and redemption:”

1 Corinthians 1:30

Answer

In finding wisdom, and wisdom has a name: Jesus. Whoever finds Him finds life and favor with God. Turning Him down isn't neutral. It wrongs your own soul. He's calling out to everyone, and anyone can come.
