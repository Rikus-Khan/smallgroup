Numbers 3

The Levites shall be mine

Instead of every firstborn son in Israel, the LORD takes one whole tribe for himself. They camp round his tent and carry his house. The firstborn left over are bought back, five shekels a head.

Part one

The sons of Aaron

Verses 1–4

Four sons were anointed. Two of them were gone within a year.

Hard words in verses 1 to 4

  1. generationsthe family record: the sons and what became of them
  2. anointedset apart with oil to be a priest
  3. consecratedmade holy, set apart for the LORD's service
  4. minister, ministeredto serve
  5. the priest’s officethe work of a priest: offering, burning incense, standing between God and the people
  6. strange firefire that the LORD had not commanded

Strange fire

The book of Leviticus tells it in three verses. Aaron's two oldest sons, anointed priests, took their censers at the start of the priesthood:

“And Nadab and Abihu, the sons of Aaron, took either of them his censer, and put fire therein, and put incense thereon, and offered strange fire before the LORD, which he commanded them not.”

Leviticus 10:1

“Then Moses said unto Aaron, This is it that the LORD spake, saying, I will be sanctified in them that come nigh me, and before all the people I will be glorified. And Aaron held his peace.”

Leviticus 10:3

The sin is in the last five words of verse 1: “which he commanded them not.” They came near to God their own way. The chapter before us says it again, calmly, and then goes on: “they had no children: and Eleazar and Ithamar ministered in the priest’s office in the sight of Aaron their father.”

Who is left standing

The sons of Aaron: Nadab and Abihu, gone. Eleazar and Ithamar, still on their feet, still at work.

Two things for a man in recovery. First, the LORD sets the way into his presence, and the way is not up to us: we do not get to come near on our own terms. Second, look at who is left standing: Aaron, who lost two sons at once and “held his peace”, and two brothers who kept serving “in the sight of Aaron their father”. The work went on. God does not quit on the men who remain.

Part two

The Levites given to Aaron

Verses 5–13

A whole tribe, taken in the place of the firstborn.

Hard words in verses 5 to 10

  1. chargethe duty put in a man's keeping
  2. the instrumentsthe vessels and tools of the tabernacle
  3. wholly givengiven altogether, with nothing held back
  4. wait onattend to, carry out
  5. the stranger that cometh nighany man who was not a priest or a Levite and who came near the holy things (as in 1:51)

Notice the word “given”. The Levites did not volunteer, and they did not earn it. “Thou shalt give the Levites unto Aaron and to his sons.” Later the LORD says the same thing to Aaron: “I have taken your brethren the Levites from among the children of Israel: to you they are given as a gift for the LORD” (18:6).

Why the firstborn were his

Matrix is an old word for the womb: “the firstborn that openeth the matrix” is the first son born to a mother. Hallowed means set apart as holy. On the night of the Passover the LORD struck every firstborn in Egypt, and the firstborn of Israel were spared by the blood on the door. From that night they were his.

The Passover night

Every firstborn is the LORD’s

Spared by the blood, so his: “mine shall they be.”

In the wilderness

The Levites in their place

One tribe given instead, to serve at the tabernacle.

So the tribe of Levi stands where the firstborn of every house should have stood. The chapter says “mine” four times, and “I am the LORD” three times.

“Sanctify unto me all the firstborn, whatsoever openeth the womb among the children of Israel, both of man and of beast: it is mine.”

Exodus 13:2

Spared, and so his

A man who was spared owes a debt he cannot pay, and the LORD does not ask him to pay it with gold. He asks for a life given to him. Paul says it to every believer:

“What? know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you, which ye have of God, and ye are not your own? For ye are bought with a price: therefore glorify God in your body, and in your spirit, which are God’s.”

1 Corinthians 6:19–20

If you came out of a life that belonged to the bottle, the needle, or another man's orders, here is the other half of the news. You were not set free to belong to nobody. You were bought, and you belong to the one who paid. That is not a chain. It is the safest place a man can be.

Verse 1 calls this “the generations of Aaron and Moses”. The priests came from one house of the Levites, and the rest of the tribe served beside them.

The Levites round the tent

Round the tabernacle itself, closest to God, and outside them the twelve tribes in their four camps (chapter 2). All the Levite males from a month old and upward were “twenty and two thousand” (verse 39). The Levites are counted from a month old, but they are set to the work from thirty years old and upward (4:3).

Part four

The firstborn bought back

Verses 40–51

A Levite for every firstborn, and five shekels for each one left over.

Hard words in verses 40 to 51

  1. threescore and thirteensixty and thirteen: 73. So “two hundred and threescore and thirteen” is 273
  2. redeemedbought back, by a price paid
  3. by the polla head at a time: five shekels for each man
  4. the shekel of the sanctuarythe standard shekel kept at the tabernacle; “the shekel is twenty gerahs”
  5. the odd numberthe few over and above: the extra 273
  6. redemption moneythe price that bought them back

Every firstborn, counted and covered

22,273firstborn males, a month old and up (verse 43)
22,000Levites, each taking one firstborn's place (verse 39)
273firstborn over and above (verse 46)
273 × 5shekels apiece, after the shekel of the sanctuary (verse 47)
1,365shekels, “a thousand three hundred and threescore and five” (verse 50)

22,273 minus 22,000 is 273, and 273 times five is 1,365. Every one of the firstborn was either stood for by a Levite or bought back with silver. Not one was left over.

Nobody left over

The LORD could have let the 273 go. They were only the few over the number. Instead he counted them, named a price, and made sure that every one was covered. The silver was a picture. The real price is named in the New Testament:

“For even the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many.”

Mark 10:45

“To the general assembly and church of the firstborn, which are written in heaven, and to God the Judge of all, and to the spirits of just men made perfect,”

Hebrews 12:23

Every believer belongs to “the church of the firstborn”: the LORD's own, ransomed, and written in heaven. If you feel like the odd one over and above, the one who barely counts, this chapter is for you. He counted the 273.

Question 1 of 6

Strange fire

Which two sons of Aaron died before the LORD, and what had they done?

What verses tell us about this?

“And Nadab and Abihu, the sons of Aaron, took either of them his censer, and put fire therein, and put incense thereon, and offered strange fire before the LORD, which he commanded them not.”

Leviticus 10:1

Answer

Nadab and Abihu. They offered strange fire before the LORD, “which he commanded them not”, and they died. Eleazar and Ithamar went on serving in the sight of their father.

Question 2 of 6

Given to Aaron

Who was brought near and given to Aaron and his sons, and for what work?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

The tribe of Levi, “wholly given” to Aaron and his sons, to keep the charge of the tabernacle and its instruments and to do the service of it.

Question 3 of 6

Instead of the firstborn

Why did the LORD take the Levites instead of all the firstborn of Israel?

What verses tell us about this?

“Sanctify unto me all the firstborn, whatsoever openeth the womb among the children of Israel, both of man and of beast: it is mine.”

Exodus 13:2

Answer

Because all the firstborn were his: on the day he smote the firstborn in Egypt he hallowed the firstborn of Israel to himself. He took the Levites in their place.

Question 4 of 6

Three families

What were the three families of the Levites, and on which side of the tabernacle did each pitch?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

Gershon on the west, behind the tabernacle; Kohath on the south; Merari on the north. On the east, before the tabernacle, were Moses, and Aaron and his sons.

Question 5 of 6

The load

What was each family to carry?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

Gershon the curtains, the tent and the hangings. Kohath the ark, the table, the candlestick, the altars and the vessels. Merari the boards, bars, pillars and sockets: the frame of the house.

Question 6 of 6

Bought back

How were the firstborn over and above the Levites redeemed, and for how much?

What verses tell us about this?

“For even the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many.”

Mark 10:45

Answer

Five shekels apiece, by the poll, for the 273 over and above the Levites: 1,365 shekels in all. The silver pictures the real ransom, the life of the Son of man.
