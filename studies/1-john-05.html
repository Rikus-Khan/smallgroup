Still John, the old apostle at Ephesus, about AD 85 to 95. The island of Patmos, where he would be sent away and see the Revelation (about AD 95), is just off the coast.

Today is the end of the letter. John will tell us in plain words why he wrote it (5:13), and then sign off with one short warning.

Part one

The victory

Verses 1–5

Born into God's family, and given a way to win against the world.

Born into a family

Whosoever believeth is born of God. That is how a man gets into God's family: not by cleaning up, but by trusting that Jesus is the Christ. And when you're born into a family, you get brothers. Love the Father, and you'll love His other children too (5:1).

Grievous means heavy, crushing. Some of us think of God's commands like a parole sheet: one slip and you're done. John says they're not grievous. They're a Father's words to a son He has already taken in.

“For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.”

Matthew 11:30

What wins

The world here is everything that pulls a man away from God: its lusts, its lies, its pressure. What beats it?

  • willpowerwe've all tried that
  • a clean streaka good thing, but it can break
  • a program, by itselfit helps, but it can't make you new
  • our faithtrusting that Jesus is the Son of God (5:5)

Faith wins because of who it holds on to. Jesus has already beaten the world (John 16:33). Faith ties a weak man to Him. Today, when the pull comes, the fight is to trust Him in that moment and do the next right thing.

Part two

The witness of God

Verses 6–13

God has spoken about His Son. Believe the record, and you can know where you stand.

Witnesses, all pointing one way

God's law wanted two or three witnesses before anything was settled. God gives more than enough.

Many Bible teachers take the water as Jesus' baptism, where the Father said, “This is my beloved Son,” and the blood as His death on the cross. He didn't only begin well. He went all the way to the blood.

And the Spirit still bears witness today, inside every believer, that it's all true.

Eternal life is not a thing you earn. It's in a Person.

We take men's word every day: a doctor, a judge, a boss who says the check is coming. “The witness of God is greater.” If He has spoken, that settles it.

Not believing Him isn't a neutral position. John says it makes God a liar. But the man who believes has the witness in himself: the Spirit inside, agreeing with the Book.

Two men, and no third

He that hath the Son→hath life
He that hath not the Son of God→hath not life

There is no third line for “he that has been good enough,” or “he that went to church a lot.” And there's no line for “he that has the Son, but messed up yesterday.” The question is only this: do you have the Son?

Not guess. Not hope. Know.

Here is why John wrote the whole letter. Not so you could hope, or guess, or wait to find out when you die. That ye may know.

Your feelings swing like that needle. Yesterday's fall pushes it back toward “I guess.” But knowing doesn't rest on your feelings or your record. It rests on the record God gave (5:11).

What your knowing stands on

  1. God's record“God hath given to us eternal life” (5:11). He said it.
  2. The blood“the blood of Jesus Christ his Son cleanseth us from all sin” (1:7).
  3. The Advocate“If any man sin, we have an advocate with the Father” (2:1).
  4. The Spirit“he hath given us of his Spirit” (4:13).

“Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and shall not come into condemnation; but is passed from death unto life.”

John 5:24

None of these four is you. That's why it holds. A believer who still sins is not shut out of this verse: the letter was written to him (2:1).

Part three

We know

Verses 14–21

Praying with confidence, praying for a brother who falls, kept by God, and one last warning.

Confidence, not a blank check

Ifwe ask according to His will,
→
thenHe hears us, and we have it.

This isn't a way to get God to sign off on whatever we want. It's better: some prayers are sure things. Pray for strength against your sin, for a clean life, for help to forgive, and you know you're asking His will.

“For this is the will of God, even your sanctification, that ye should abstain from fornication:”

1 Thessalonians 4:3

For the things you're not sure of (a job, a case, a family member), ask boldly, then pray the way Jesus did: “not as I will, but as thou wilt” (Matthew 26:39).

A hard verse, held humbly

The “sin unto death” is one of the hard places in the Bible, and good teachers differ on it. The two main views:

Many teachers

Death as discipline

A believer who keeps on in a sin may be taken home early by God: his body dies, but he is not lost. “many sleep” (1 Corinthians 11:30).

Others

Rejecting the Son

The settled refusal of Christ by men like the false teachers who walked out (2:19). There is no life for the man who will not have the Son (5:12).

What's plain is enough. It is not a believer losing his salvation. It is not the one sin you're afraid you did. A man worried he's committed it is showing a tender heart toward God, and that is the Spirit's work in him. The command for us is simple: see a brother fall, and pray for him.

Sinneth not

Same as chapter 3: a man born of God does not keep on in sin as his way of life. He still falls. John already said so (1:8–9). But he doesn't live there, and he can't settle down there.

Toucheth here means to lay hold of, to grab. The devil can tempt a believer, but he can't take hold of him and drag him back as his own.

“The whole world lieth in wickedness,” like a man asleep in the devil's lap. You were there once. You're not now.

John's last word: know

Count it. In this last chapter John says know eight times, seven of them in the last nine verses.

13×1 14  15×2 16  17  18×1 19×1 20×2 21 

We know He hears us. We know the born-again man is kept. We know we are of God. We know the Son of God is come. An old man at the end of his life isn't guessing. And he wants you not to guess either.

His Son Jesus Christ“This is the true God, and eternal life” (5:20)

An idol is anything that sits in God's place: what you run to when it hurts, what you can't say no to, what you'd give anything to keep.

Keep yourselves

These believers lived in Ephesus, home of the great temple of Diana. Every street had idols for sale. Some of us know that city: a different idol on every corner, and all of them promising relief.

“For they themselves shew of us what manner of entering in we had unto you, and how ye turned to God from idols to serve the living and true God;”

1 Thessalonians 1:9

Keep yourselves is something you do. God keeps you (5:18), and you guard the seat. Today: name the one thing that keeps trying to sit there. Tell one brother what it is. Then when it shows up, turn it away and turn to the true God.

Points to remember

  1. Victory“This is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith” 5:4Not willpower. Faith that holds on to Jesus, who has already won.
  2. Witness“The witness of God is greater” 5:9The Father, the Word and the Holy Ghost, and the spirit, the water and the blood, all point to Jesus.
  3. Know“That ye may know that ye have eternal life” 5:13Not guess, not hope. He that hath the Son hath life, and can know it.
  4. Prayer“If we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us” 5:14Pray His will with confidence. Some prayers are sure things.
  5. A brother's sin“He shall ask, and he shall give him life” 5:16When a brother falls, pray for him. A believer doesn't live in sin, and the wicked one can't take hold of him.
  6. Idols“Little children, keep yourselves from idols” 5:21Guard God's place in your life. Nothing else gets the seat.

Question 1 of 6

Victory

What is the victory that overcometh the world?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

Our faith: believing that Jesus is the Son of God. Everyone born of God overcomes the world, not by willpower, but by trusting the One who has already beaten it.

Question 2 of 6

Witness

Who bears witness that Jesus is the Son of God?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

God Himself. In heaven, the Father, the Word and the Holy Ghost. In earth, the spirit, the water and the blood. And God's witness is greater than any man's.

Question 3 of 6

Know

Can a believer know that he has eternal life?

What verses tell us about this?

“And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand.”

John 10:28

Answer

Yes. John wrote the letter so that we may know. He that hath the Son hath life. It rests on God's record, not on our feelings or our last week.

Question 4 of 6

Prayer

What confidence do we have when we pray?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

That if we ask anything according to His will, He hears us, and we have what we asked. It isn't a blank check. But when we pray for what God has already said He wants, like strength against sin, we can be sure.

Question 5 of 6

A brother's sin

What should we do when we see a brother sin?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

Pray for him. The sin unto death is hard, and teachers differ, but it doesn't mean a believer loses his salvation. A man born of God doesn't keep on in sin as a way of life, and the wicked one can't take hold of him.

Question 6 of 6

Idols

What is John's last word to his little children?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

Keep yourselves from idols. Jesus Christ is the true God and eternal life, so nothing else gets His place: not a bottle, not money, not pride, not anything.
