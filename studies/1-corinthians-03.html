1 Corinthians 3

God gives the growth, and the fire tests the work

Two preachers, one church that picked sides. Paul's answer: neither of us is the point. Build on Jesus, build with care, and remember the building is God's.

Corinth was a rough, rich port, and its church was a mess. Chapters 1 to 4 deal with the first problem: believers lining up behind their favorite teacher. “I am of Paul; and I of Apollos.” Chapter 3 is Paul's answer.

Read this chapter alone and it still stands: God does the growing, Jesus is the only foundation, and the fire tests what we build. Chapter 4 finishes the thought.

Part one

Babes, still

Verses 1–4

A newborn on milk is a joy. A grown man still on a bottle has stopped growing.

Three years and still milk

  1. about AD 50Paul comes to CorinthActs 18
  2. a year and six monthsHe teaches them dailyActs 18:11
  3. about AD 52He leaves; Apollos followsActs 18:24–19:1
  4. about AD 55This letter: still babes“ye are yet carnal”

“As newborn babes, desire the sincere milk of the word, that ye may grow thereby:”

1 Peter 2:2

Milk is not a bad thing. Every new believer starts there, and God is glad to feed him. The trouble is staying there.

“Carnal” does not mean “lost”

Walking as men

Carnal

Living by the old appetite: envying, strife, divisions. Jealous of the next man's gift, keeping score, picking a side.

Still true of them

Brethren, in Christ

Paul calls them brethren and babes in Christ. They are saved. They are just acting like they aren't.

That is good news for a man who fell yesterday. Acting like the old man does not turn you back into him. God still says in Christ. But it does cost you: it keeps you on milk, and it keeps the church fighting.

“For every one that useth milk is unskilful in the word of righteousness: for he is a babe. But strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age, even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil.”

Hebrews 5:13–14

Meat comes by use: you grow by doing, a day at a time. Not by a feeling, and not by one big night.

Part two

God gave the increase

Verses 5–9

Paul and Apollos are not rival teams. They are two hands on the same field.

Planted, watered, grown

Paul planted the church in Corinth (Acts 18). Apollos came after and taught it more (Acts 18:24–28). Both mattered. Neither could make one thing grow.

Where Apollos came from

Apollos was a Jew from Alexandria in Egypt, a great city of books and learning. A gifted speaker, “mighty in the scriptures” (Acts 18:24). He crossed the sea to Ephesus, and from there on to Corinth.

Paul had planted. Apollos came to water. Some in Corinth liked Paul's plain way better; some were dazzled by Apollos. Paul and Apollos were friends, and neither one asked for a fan club.

One crew, one boss

  • “Paul is better”Paul would be the first to say: I only planted
  • “Apollos is better”he only watered
  • God, who gives the increasethe One who makes anything grow at all

Labourers together with God, and God's husbandry (His farm), God's building. You are not the owner of your growth, and you do not have to be. You do your part: show up, plant, water. He does what only He can.

And every man shall receive his own reward according to his own labour. God sees the quiet work: the man who gets up and goes to the job, the man who sat with a hurting friend. Nobody's labor goes missing.

Part three

The foundation and the fire

Verses 10–17

One foundation, already laid. And every man gets to choose what he builds on it.

One foundation

  • my clean daysthey are a good thing to build, but they are not what holds the house up
  • my church recordit can't hold a man up
  • how bad I was, or how goodneither one is the foundation
  • Jesus Christalready laid: His death and rising for you

A masterbuilder is the head builder. The foundation goes in first, and it has to be right, because everything stands on it. Paul laid it in Corinth by preaching Jesus. It was never something the Corinthians could improve.

“Therefore thus saith the Lord God, Behold, I lay in Zion for a foundation a stone, a tried stone, a precious corner stone, a sure foundation: he that believeth shall not make haste.”

Isaiah 28:16

Tried by fire

Click, and let the fire come. Both builders used the same foundation. Only one tower stays standing.

What does that mean?

The foundation

Settled

Salvation. A gift, laid by Christ. He himself shall be saved. The fire does not touch it, and it cannot be tested away.

The building

Judged

What you built on it: your work, your service, how you treated people. Gold or stubble, and God sees which.

“There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.”

Romans 8:1

So this is a day of reward, not a day of rescue. Paul says plainly that a man can have a lot burn and still be saved. Your salvation can't burn. Your work can, and it matters, which is why it's worth building well.

“For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ; that every one may receive the things done in his body, according to that he hath done, whether it be good or bad.”

2 Corinthians 5:10

Gold, or stubble?

Paul is mostly talking about men who teach and lead, but the test reaches anyone building a life on Jesus. Here is a plain way to ask it.

Burns up

Wood, hay, stubble

Done to be seen. Done to win a side. Done to look big, or to get paid back. Quick, cheap, and for me.

Comes through

Gold, silver, stones

Done for Christ, and for the person in front of you. The honest day's work. The call you made when no one was watching.

If you look at your past and see a lot of stubble, you are in good company. Everyone does. The foundation is still there, and so is today's course of bricks. You can lay gold on top of a bad year.

What is the next brick you're laying? A phone call you owe. A day at work done right. A man you're helping stay clean.

God's address

a building of stone → you The Corinthians knew temples: Corinth was full of them, built for idols. God's own house is no longer a building. His Spirit lives in His people, from the day each one believes. Nobody has to earn it, or wait for a second experience.

“What? know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you, which ye have of God, and ye are not your own?”

1 Corinthians 6:19

A hard verse, honestly

“If any man defile the temple of God, him shall God destroy.” It sounds like a trapdoor under every man who has slipped. Look at who Paul has in view.

Not this verse

The man who fell yesterday

If you hate what you did and you're back on your knees, that's the Spirit at work in you. Confess it. He is faithful and just to forgive.

This verse

The man who tears up the church

The ye is plural: the church together is the temple. Paul is warning the men who split it into camps for their own pride.

God takes His church seriously, and the man who wrecks it answers for it. Good teachers differ on exactly what destroy means. What is plain is the target, the man ruining the church, and that the rest of the Bible says His own are held fast (John 10:28).

“And are built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone; In whom all the building fitly framed together groweth unto an holy temple in the Lord: In whom ye also are builded together for an habitation of God through the Spirit.”

Ephesians 2:20–22

Part four

Glory in no man

Verses 18–23

The smartest-looking man in the room is not the point. Everything is yours, and you are Christ's.

Street smart, God smart

The old voiceNever let anyone see you don't know. Never admit you were wrong. Be the sharpest guy in the room.

PaulLet no man deceive himself. If any man among you seemeth to be wise in this world, let him become a fool, that he may be wise. teachable

Become a fool doesn't mean act stupid. It means drop the act: admit what you don't know, and let God teach you. The man who thinks he has it all worked out is the easiest man to deceive, and most often the one doing the deceiving is himself.

Craftiness is cunning. Paul quotes Job: God catches the clever in their own tricks (Job 5:13). Anyone who has tried to talk his way out of a corner knows how that goes.

All are yours, and you are Christ's

Why pick a favorite teacher when all are yours? Paul, Apollos and Cephas (that is Peter) are gifts to you, not rivals for your loyalty. Even death is on the list: it can't take you out of Christ's hand.

The ring that matters most: ye are Christ's; and Christ is God's. You don't belong to a teacher, a gang, or your old habit. You belong to Christ, and that is a safe place to be.

Points to remember

  1. Milk and meat“I have fed you with milk, and not with meat” 3:2They were saved, but still living like the old man. New believers start on milk; the trouble is never leaving it.
  2. Growth“God gave the increase” 3:6We plant and water. God makes it grow, and the credit is His.
  3. Foundation“Which is Jesus Christ” 3:11The only foundation, already laid. It's not our record, good or bad.
  4. The fire“He himself shall be saved; yet so as by fire” 3:15Our work will be tested. Some may burn, but the man stays saved. Build with gold.
  5. The temple“Ye are the temple of God” 3:16God's Spirit lives in His people, and in His church together. Handle both with care.
  6. Boasting“Let no man glory in men” 3:21All things are ours, and we are Christ's. No teacher is worth a split.

Question 1 of 6

Milk and meat

Why couldn't Paul feed these believers solid food?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

They were still carnal: babes in Christ, full of envy, strife and divisions. They were saved, but they weren't growing. Milk is right for a new believer. Staying on it is the trouble.

Question 2 of 6

Growth

Who makes a believer grow?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

God does. Paul planted, Apollos watered, and God gave the increase. We are labourers together with Him, and each gets his own reward, but the growing is God's.

Question 3 of 6

Foundation

What is the one foundation?

What verses tell us about this?

“And are built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone;”

Ephesians 2:20

Answer

Jesus Christ. Nobody can lay another. It has already been laid for every man who will trust Him, and we only choose what to build on top of it.

Question 4 of 6

The fire

What happens to a believer's work when the fire tries it?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

The fire shows what sort it is. Work that lasts gets a reward. Work that burns is lost, but the man himself is saved. A believer's works are judged, and his salvation stays secure.

Question 5 of 6

The temple

Whose temple are we?

What verses tell us about this?

“What? know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you, which ye have of God, and ye are not your own?”

1 Corinthians 6:19

Answer

God's. His Spirit lives in every believer, and in His church together. That makes the church holy ground, and a thing to guard, not to split.

Question 6 of 6

Boasting

Why should no man glory in men?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

Because all things are already ours, and we belong to Christ, who belongs to God. Teachers are gifts, not teams to join. Only God deserves the glory.
