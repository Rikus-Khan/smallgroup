Numbers 34

The borders of the land, and the men who divide it

Before Israel crosses Jordan, the LORD draws the edges of the land and names the men who will share it out: a priest, a captain, and a prince from every tribe. One of them is Caleb, who saw the land forty years before.

Part one

“The land of Canaan with the coasts thereof”

Verses 1–15

Four borders, named from the sea to the river.

A land with edges

  • the land of Canaanthe land promised to Abraham, now to be given to his children
  • fall unto youcome to you, by lot
  • inheritancewhat a family receives and keeps: not wages, not earned
  • the coasts thereofits borders: here, coast means a border, not only a seashore

God gave Abraham the land in a word, and gives it here with its edges drawn. A promise with borders is a promise a man can hold the LORD to.

“And I will give unto thee, and to thy seed after thee, the land wherein thou art a stranger, all the land of Canaan, for an everlasting possession; and I will be their God.”

Genesis 17:8

The word border comes fourteen times in this chapter. The mount Hor of verse 7 is on the north border, “from the great sea”. It is not the Hor where Aaron died, which stood “in the edge of the land of Edom” (33:37).

Names we read, and do not pin

  • On the souththe ascent of Akrabbim, Hazar-addar, Azmon, the river of Egypt
  • On the northZedad, Ziphron, Hazar-enan
  • On the eastShepham, Riblah “on the east side of Ain”

Moses gives these as names, and the text does not say where the stones stood. So there are no dots for them on our maps. What the text does fix is enough: the great sea on the west, Jordan and the salt sea on the east, the sea of Chinnereth (Luke calls it “the lake of Gennesaret”, Luke 5:1), and a wilderness on the south, “along by the coast of Edom”.

Nine and a half, two and a half

9½tribes to receive by lot
2½already have theirs, east of Jordan
12tribes with land

Reuben, Gad and half of Manasseh asked for the east side in chapter 32. The rest cross the river for theirs. Levi has no share of the land at all: “I am thy part and thine inheritance among the children of Israel” (18:20).

Drawn by a man who would not go in

Moses is the one telling Israel where every border runs, and the LORD had already told him he would not cross.

“And the LORD said unto Moses, Get thee up into this mount Abarim, and see the land which I have given unto the children of Israel.”

Numbers 27:12

“And the LORD said unto him, This is the land which I sware unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob, saying, I will give it unto thy seed: I have caused thee to see it with thine eyes, but thou shalt not go over thither.”

Deuteronomy 34:4

He draws the border and names the men, and he does it faithfully. A man can do the work God gave him for a land that he will only see. Moses' sin at the rock was real (20:12), and so was the job he still had to do.

One prince from every tribe: ten names, since nine tribes and half a tribe are still to receive. Each man is named with his father, so no one is a face in a crowd. Two of them, Shemuel of Simeon and Pedahel of Naphtali, have fathers named Ammihud: two men of two tribes with the same name. “The prince of the children of Joseph, for the tribe of the children of Manasseh” is Hanniel (verse 23).

The two who came through

“Doubtless ye shall not come into the land, concerning which I sware to make you dwell therein, save Caleb the son of Jephunneh, and Joshua the son of Nun.”

Numbers 14:30

“For the LORD had said of them, They shall surely die in the wilderness. And there was not left a man of them, save Caleb the son of Jephunneh, and Joshua the son of Nun.”

Numbers 26:65

Of all the men numbered at Sinai, these two were left. Both stand here: Joshua “the son of Nun” (he was Oshea of Ephraim among the spies, 13:8, 16), with the priest, and Caleb of Judah among the princes. Forty years on, the men who stood against the fear of the rest were the men who divided the land.

Of the twelve men Moses sent to spy out the land (13:4–15), only Caleb is on this list of princes. The other nine princes are men of the new generation, named here for the first time.

And so they did

“And these are the countries which the children of Israel inherited in the land of Canaan, which Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the heads of the fathers of the tribes of the children of Israel, distributed for inheritance to them.”

Joshua 14:1

“And the LORD gave unto Israel all the land which he sware to give unto their fathers; and they possessed it, and dwelt therein.”

Joshua 21:43

Eleazar and Joshua did what chapter 34 named them to do, with the heads of the tribes beside them. The names and the borders were not a plan on paper: they were done.

Real world: your portion

Every tribe got its own lot, and none of them chose it. For a man in recovery, the word is the same: there is a place for you.

  • SinWe measure our lot against another man's: his job, his wife, his clean years. That is envy, and it steals the day.
  • RegretYou may feel you wasted the land: the years, the money, the people. The tribes were not told to earn the land back, only to receive it.
  • GraceAn inheritance is not wages. The Father “hath made us meet to be partakers of the inheritance of the saints in light” (Colossians 1:12).
  • Hope“The lines are fallen unto me in pleasant places; yea, I have a goodly heritage” (Psalm 16:6). The LORD himself is the portion.
  • TodayName one thing in your own lot to thank God for, and say it to a brother in Christ. Then stop comparing for the rest of the day.

Points to remember

  1. The land“this is the land that shall fall unto you for an inheritance, even the land of Canaan with the coasts thereof” 34:2The land is an inheritance, with its edges drawn: a gift, not wages.
  2. The borders“ye shall even have the great sea for a border: this shall be your west border” 34:6The sea on the west, Jordan and the salt sea on the east: named before they crossed.
  3. The nine and a half“which the LORD commanded to give unto the nine tribes, and to the half tribe” 34:13By lot, and by the LORD's command, not by a man's choice.
  4. The two and a half“have received their inheritance on this side Jordan near Jericho eastward” 34:15Reuben, Gad and half of Manasseh already had theirs, east of the river.
  5. The men“Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun” 34:17A priest, a captain and a prince from every tribe: nobody divided it alone.
  6. The old spy“Of the tribe of Judah, Caleb the son of Jephunneh” 34:19Caleb and Joshua, the two who came through the forty years, share out the land they once saw.

Question 1 of 6

The land

What was the land called, and how was it to come to Israel?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

The land of Canaan, with the coasts thereof: an inheritance, which they were to inherit by lot, as the LORD commanded.

Question 2 of 6

The borders

What was the west border, and where did the east border end?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

The great sea was the west border. The east border came down by the sea of Chinnereth, went down to Jordan, and ended at the salt sea.

Question 3 of 6

The nine and a half

How many tribes were to receive the land by lot?

What verses tell us about this?

“By lot was their inheritance, as the LORD commanded by the hand of Moses, for the nine tribes, and for the half tribe.”

Joshua 14:2

Answer

Nine tribes and the half tribe, by lot, as the LORD commanded to give.

Question 4 of 6

The two and a half

Which tribes had already received their inheritance, and where?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

Reuben, Gad and half the tribe of Manasseh: the two tribes and the half tribe, on this side Jordan near Jericho, eastward, toward the sunrising.

Question 5 of 6

The men

Who were to divide the land among the tribes?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

Eleazar the priest and Joshua the son of Nun, with one prince of every tribe.

Question 6 of 6
