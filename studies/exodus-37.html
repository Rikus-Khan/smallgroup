Made of gold, made as told

Today's chapter, inside the tent

These are the pieces the priests would see: the table and the lampstand, the altar of incense, and behind the vail the ark. Each was commanded in chapters 25 and 30. Now Bezaleel makes them.

We are reading the building done, not the building planned. Watch for one thing: how exactly it matches.

Part one

“Bezaleel made the ark”

Verses 1–9

The chest that holds the law, and the lid of gold with two cherubims.

The ark and its lid

  • shittim woodacacia, a hard desert wood
  • pure goldthe wood covered in gold, inside and out
  • crowna gold rim running round the top edge
  • stavespoles slid through the four rings to carry it, never by hand
  • mercy seatthe lid of pure gold

The two cherubims were “beaten out of one piece” with the lid: not stuck on, but one metal. Their wings covered the mercy seat, and their faces turned toward it.

The law underneath, the mercy on top

The ark: the testimony inside“Thou shalt put into the ark the testimony which I shall give thee” (25:16), the tables of the ten commandments
The mercy seat: the lid on the lawpure gold, one piece with the cherubims, covering the law below
The blood, on the mercy seatonce a year the high priest sprinkled it there (Leviticus 16:14–15)
God meets His people above it“there I will meet with thee, and I will commune with thee from above the mercy seat” (25:22)

Under the lid lay the law, which every man had broken. On top of the lid, blood. And above the blood, the LORD. He did not meet them over the law. He met them over the mercy.

“A propitiation”

“For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God; Being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus: Whom God hath set forth to be a propitiation through faith in his blood, to declare his righteousness for the remission of sins that are past, through the forbearance of God;”

Romans 3:23–25

Propitiation is the sacrifice that turns away God's wrath. In the Greek, the word Paul uses is the same one used for the mercy seat. God set forth Jesus as the true mercy seat: the place where a guilty man and a holy God meet, over the blood.

You do not meet God by hiding the law you broke. You meet Him over the blood that covers it. If you have failed again, the answer is not to pretend. It is to go back to the mercy seat, and to tell a brother.

Part two

“The table” and “the candlestick”

Verses 10–24

Bread and light, set out before the LORD.

The table, and its bread

  • the table3 feet long, 18 inches wide, 27 inches high, covered in gold
  • border of an handbreadtha rim about the width of a hand (three inches or so), to keep things from sliding off
  • dishes, spoons, bowls, coversthe vessels of pure gold that went with it
  • shewbread“show bread”: the bread set out on the table “before me alway” (25:30)

The table is a place of provision. Bread, always there, set before God.

“And Jesus said unto them, I am the bread of life: he that cometh to me shall never hunger; and he that believeth on me shall never thirst.”

John 6:35

Jesus said the bread of life is Himself. A man who comes to Him is fed, and he does not need to carry his own supply.

One piece of beaten gold

  • candlesticka lampstand: it held seven oil lamps
  • knopsornamental knobs or buds, with “flowers” and almond-shaped bowls
  • snuffers, snuffdishestongs for trimming the wicks, and trays for the burnt ends
  • a talenta heavy weight, about 75 pounds of pure gold for the lampstand and its tools

Six branches, three on each side of a central shaft, make seven lamps. “All of it was one beaten work of pure gold”: not cast and joined, but hammered from a single mass.

The light in the dark tent

The tent had no windows. The only light in the holy place was this lampstand, tended night after night. Without it, the priests would have worked in the dark.

“Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.”

John 8:12

Bread and light, every day: the two things a man needs and cannot make himself. The group meets daily for the same reason. You do not get yesterday's bread, and you do not walk by yesterday's light.

If you are walking in the dark today, you do not need to feel your way. Go to the light, and bring a brother with you.

Part three

“The incense altar”

Verses 25–29

The small gold altar, and the oil and incense made for it.

The altar of incense

  • foursquaresquare: a cubit by a cubit (18 inches), and two cubits (3 feet) high
  • hornsa point at each top corner, “of the same” wood and gold
  • incensesweet spice burned for its smoke and scent
  • apothecarya perfumer: the oil and incense were made by the craft of one who blends spices

It stood just in front of the vail, close to the ark (30:6), and incense was burned on it every morning and every evening (30:7).

“Let my prayer be set forth before thee as incense; and the lifting up of my hands as the evening sacrifice.”

Psalm 141:2

David asks that his prayer go up like incense. Not every prayer is eloquent. A short, honest one counts: “Lord, I'm struggling.” It rises like sweet smoke.

Real world: done as told

This chapter is a list of measurements: a cubit and a half, a talent of gold. It does not read like a story. But look at the one who did it. Bezaleel took a pattern he had not drawn, and made it exactly.

  • Obedience follows mercyThese are the people who built a calf. The LORD forgave them first, and then they did exactly as told. They did not do it to earn their way back.
  • Small things, done rightHardly anyone would ever see the ark. Only the high priest went in, once a year. Bezaleel made it as well as if the whole camp were watching. A man who is faithful when nobody is watching is a man others can lean on.
  • God's house is usThe Spirit of God now lives in the believer himself.

“Know ye not that ye are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you?”

1 Corinthians 3:16

If God cared that much about how a golden lampstand was made, how much more does He care about you, His temple. Take the next small step He has shown you, and do it as told. Then tell a brother you did.

Points to remember

  1. The ark“And Bezaleel made the ark of shittim wood” 37:1Wood and gold, carried by staves. It held the law the people had broken.
  2. The mercy seat“And he made the mercy seat of pure gold” 37:6The lid over the law. God met His people over the blood, not over the law. Jesus is our mercy seat.
  3. The table“And he made the table of shittim wood” 37:10Bread always before the LORD. Jesus is the bread of life.
  4. The lampstand“And he made the candlestick of pure gold: of beaten work” 37:17One beaten piece, seven lamps. Light in a tent with no windows.
  5. The incense altar“And he made the incense altar of shittim wood” 37:25Square, gold-covered, before the vail. Prayer rises like incense.
  6. The oil and incense“And he made the holy anointing oil, and the pure incense of sweet spices, according to the work of the apothecary” 37:29Made as a skilled perfumer would make them, as the LORD told him.

Question 1 of 6

The ark

What did Bezaleel make the ark of, and how was it carried?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

Of shittim wood, overlaid with pure gold inside and out, with a gold crown. It was carried by staves of wood and gold put through four gold rings at its corners.

Question 2 of 6

The mercy seat

What was made on top of the ark, and which way did the cherubims face?

What verses tell us about this?

“Whom God hath set forth to be a propitiation through faith in his blood, to declare his righteousness for the remission of sins that are past, through the forbearance of God;”

Romans 3:25

Answer

The mercy seat, a lid of pure gold, with two cherubims of one piece with it. Their faces looked toward each other and down at the mercy seat. Jesus is the true mercy seat, through faith in His blood.

Question 3 of 6

The table

What did he make for the table, and how was it carried?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

A table of shittim wood overlaid with gold, with a crown and a border, carried on staves, and vessels of pure gold: dishes, spoons, bowls and covers. The bread of the presence sat on it, and Jesus is the bread of life.

Question 4 of 6

The lampstand

What was the candlestick made of, and how many lamps did it hold?

What verses tell us about this?

“Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.”

John 8:12

Answer

Pure gold, all of it one beaten piece, with six branches and seven lamps, made from a talent of gold. Jesus is the light of the world, and the one who follows Him will not walk in the dark.

Question 5 of 6

The incense altar

What did he make to burn incense on, and what shape was it?

What verses tell us about this?

“Let my prayer be set forth before thee as incense; and the lifting up of my hands as the evening sacrifice.”

Psalm 141:2

Answer

The incense altar, of shittim wood overlaid with gold: foursquare, a cubit each way and two cubits high, with horns of the same. Prayer goes up before God like its smoke.

Question 6 of 6

The oil and incense

What else did he make, and whose craft was it done by?

What verses tell us about this?

“Know ye not that ye are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you?”

1 Corinthians 3:16

Answer

The holy anointing oil and the pure incense of sweet spices, made according to the work of the apothecary, a skilled perfumer. Done as told, down to the last detail, by men who were forgiven first.
