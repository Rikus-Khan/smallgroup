In chapter 19 heaven opened, and the King of kings rode out. The beast and the false prophet were taken and cast into the lake of fire.

One enemy is left: the one behind them all, the dragon. This chapter finishes him. Then every man who ever lived stands before God, and the books are opened.

Part one

The dragon chained

Verses 1–3

No great battle. One angel, one key, one chain.

One angel, one chain

Look who does it. Not Christ Himself, not an army: “an angel.” The devil is not God's equal, fighting Him for the world. He's a creature, and one of God's servants carries him off like a prisoner.

Shut him up, and set a seal upon him: locked, sealed, under guard. You've seen a cell door slam. This time it's the jailer of men's souls who goes in.

Whatever else the thousand years means (we'll look at that), here's the point: the devil is on God's leash, and God holds the key.

Four names, one enemy

  1. the dragonhis fury and his power (12:3, 9)
  2. that old serpentthe liar in the garden (Genesis 3)
  3. the DevilGreek diabolos: the slanderer
  4. SatanHebrew for the adversary, the accuser

From the first book of the Bible to the last, it's the same enemy. In the garden God promised that the woman's Seed would “bruise thy head” (Genesis 3:15). Here is the promise coming true.

“And the God of peace shall bruise Satan under your feet shortly. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen.”

Romans 16:20

His one trick

Chained, he can't do the thing he does best: deceive. Jesus called him “a liar, and the father of it” (John 8:44). You know his voice:

  • “One won't hurt.”
  • “Nobody will ever know.”
  • “You're too far gone. God's done with you.”
  • “…greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world.”1 John 4:4

A lie only has power while you believe it. Say the truth out loud, and tell a brother. “Resist the devil, and he will flee from you” (James 4:7).

Part two

They reigned with Christ

Verses 4–6

The men the world put to death sit on thrones. The second death can't touch them.

From the chopping block to a throne

Rome beheaded Christians who wouldn't bow to Caesar. To the world they were criminals and dead men. Look where they are now: on thrones, and “judgment was given unto them.”

Some of you have been on the wrong end of a courtroom. You know what it is to have the world say what you are. In God's courtroom, the people who stood for Jesus end up on the bench.

And they “lived.” Death didn't end their story, and it won't end yours. For a believer, the worst thing that can happen to him is not the last thing that happens to him.

Born once, die twice. Born twice, die once.

Born once

  1. born
  2. dies
  3. the second death (20:14)

Born twice

  1. born
  2. born again (John 3:3)
  3. dies, or is caught up
  4. “the second death hath no power” (20:6)

That's an old preacher's saying, and it's true. The second death is the lake of fire (20:14). A man who has been born again may still die once, but the second death has no power over him. That's why he's “blessed and holy.”

The thousand years: how good teachers read it

“A thousand years” comes six times in verses 2–7, and Bible-believing Baptists don't all read it the same way. Here are the main views, side by side:

The viewWhat it saysWho holds it
Christ comes firstpremillennialJesus returns (chapter 19), then reigns on the earth for a literal thousand years while Satan is bound. Then comes the last judgment.Many Baptists, and many Bible teachers today
The thousand years is nowamillennialThe number pictures the whole time between Christ's first and second comings. He reigns now from heaven, and Satan is bound so the gospel can reach the nations.Other Baptists, and many teachers through church history
The gospel wins firstpostmillennialThe gospel spreads until much of the world is won, a long age of blessing, and then Christ returns.Some Baptists, more so in past centuries
All of them agree: Satan loses. Christ reigns. Every man will stand before God. And those written in the Lamb's book are safe.

What is plain

Godly men differ on the order of events. Nobody has to win that argument today. Here is what the chapter makes plain to every reader:

  1. Satan loses“cast into the lake of fire” (20:10)
  2. Christ reigns“reigned with Christ” (20:4)
  3. Every man stands“small and great, stand before God” (20:12)
  4. The book is safe“written in the Lamb’s book of life” (21:27)

“For he must reign, till he hath put all enemies under his feet. The last enemy that shall be destroyed is death.”

1 Corinthians 15:25–26

Part three

Loosed a little season

Verses 7–10

The chain comes off for a little while. Then the devil goes where he can never deceive anyone again.

The problem was never the neighborhood

Gog and Magog are Ezekiel's names for nations gathered against God's people (Ezekiel 38:2). Here they stand for the whole rebellious world.

After a long age of Christ's rule, the moment the deceiver is let out, crowds follow him again. A better setting doesn't change a heart. A new address, a new job, even a prison term didn't fix the man inside.

“A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh.”

Ezekiel 36:26

Only God changes a heart. And the war ends without a fight: “fire came down from God.”

The end of the accuser

  1. cast outinto the earth (12:9)
  2. boundin the bottomless pit (20:3)
  3. looseda little season (20:7)
  4. cast into the lakefor ever and ever (20:10)

That's the last we ever hear of him. And notice who that fire was made for: “everlasting fire, prepared for the devil and his angels” (Matthew 25:41). God never built it for men. He sent His Son so no man has to go there.

Jesus died “that through death he might destroy him that had the power of death, that is, the devil” (Hebrews 2:14). The voice that accuses you day and night will one day be silent for ever.

Part four

The great white throne

Verses 11–15

Heaven and earth run from His face. Every man stands, and the books are opened.

The books, and the book

Daniel saw this court too (Daniel 7:10). The books hold every man's record, and the judgment is “according to their works”: exact and fair.

But nobody is saved by his record. “For by grace are ye saved through faith… Not of works” (Ephesians 2:8–9).

So one book decides it: the book of life. A man's name goes in it when he trusts the Lamb, and Jesus promised, “I will not blot out his name” (3:5).

Nowhere to run

Even the earth and the sky run from His face, and “there was found no place for them.” There's no lawyer, no plea deal, no back door. “Small and great”: the president and the homeless man stand in the same line.

“And as it is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment:”

Hebrews 9:27

Does a believer stand here to be condemned? Many Bible teachers, Baptists among them, say no: Jesus said a believer “shall not come into condemnation; but is passed from death unto life” (John 5:24). Believers will give account at “the judgment seat of Christ” (2 Corinthians 5:10), for reward, not for hell.

The last enemy

Nobody is missed: the sea gives up its dead, and the grave gives up its dead. Hell here is the place of the dead, and it's emptied. Then death itself is thrown away. There will be no more funerals, ever.

This is sober, and Scripture doesn't dress it up. The lake of fire is real, and it is “the second death.” God takes it so seriously that He gave His own Son to keep men out of it:

“The Lord is not slack concerning his promise, as some men count slackness; but is longsuffering to us-ward, not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.”

2 Peter 3:9

Is your name in the book?

Revelation 20:15

And whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire.

Revelation 22:17

And let him that is athirst come. And whosoever will, let him take the water of life freely.

The same word, whosoever, stands at both doors. Nobody is written in for being good, and no man is too bad to be written in. A man's name goes in the Lamb's book when he calls on Jesus and trusts Him:

“For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.”

Romans 10:13

If you've done that, your name is written, and it stays written: “rejoice, because your names are written in heaven” (Luke 10:20). If you haven't, “behold, now is the day of salvation” (2 Corinthians 6:2). Not tomorrow. Today.

Take it to work today

20:3Name the lie

When the old voice says “one won't hurt” or “you're too far gone,” call it what it is: a lie from a chained enemy.

20:12Settle the book

If you're not sure your name is written, settle it with God today. Call on Him. Ask a brother to pray with you.

20:15Tell one man

Somebody at work isn't in the book yet. Pray for him by name, and be ready to tell him about the Lamb.

Points to remember

  1. Satan“The devil that deceived them was cast into the lake of fire” 20:10He is bound by one angel, let out for a little season, and then finished for ever.
  2. A thousand years“They lived and reigned with Christ” 20:4Good teachers differ on the order, but it is plain that Satan loses, Christ reigns, every man stands before God, and those in the Lamb's book are safe.
  3. Blessed“On such the second death hath no power” 20:6Born once, die twice; born twice, die once. The lake of fire has no claim on him.
  4. The heart“And shall go out to deceive the nations” 20:8The nations follow the deceiver again: a better world doesn't change a heart. Only God does.
  5. The throne“Judged every man according to their works” 20:13Every man, small and great, is judged fairly by what he did. Nobody is saved by his record.
  6. The book“Whosoever was not found written in the book of life” 20:15The book of life is what matters, and a man's name is written there by faith in the Lamb.

Question 1 of 6

Satan

What happens to Satan in this chapter?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

An angel binds him with a great chain and shuts him in the bottomless pit for a thousand years, so he can't deceive the nations. Then he is loosed a little season, deceives the nations again, and is cast into the lake of fire for ever. He loses, completely.

Answer

Good Baptists read the order differently. But every view agrees: Satan loses, Christ reigns, every man will stand before God, and those in the Lamb's book of life are safe.

Question 3 of 6

Blessed

Why is the man who has part in the first resurrection blessed?

What verses tell us about this?

“Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and shall not come into condemnation; but is passed from death unto life.”

John 5:24

Answer

Because the second death, the lake of fire, has no power over him. He will be a priest of God and of Christ and reign with Him. Born once, die twice; born twice, die once.

Question 4 of 6

The heart

What do the nations do as soon as Satan is loosed?

What verses tell us about this?

“A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh.”

Ezekiel 36:26

Answer

They follow his lies again and gather, as many as the sand of the sea, to fight God's people. It shows that a better world doesn't change the human heart. Only God gives a new heart. And the rebellion ends with fire from heaven.

Question 5 of 6

The throne

How are the dead judged at the great white throne?

What verses tell us about this?

“For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast.”

Ephesians 2:8–9

Answer

Small and great stand before God, and every man is judged by the books, according to his works, exactly and fairly. But no one is saved by works. The book of life decides it, and a name gets there by grace through faith.

Question 6 of 6

The book

How does a man's name get written in the book of life?

What verses tell us about this?

“For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.”

Romans 10:13

“Notwithstanding in this rejoice not, that the spirits are subject unto you; but rather rejoice, because your names are written in heaven.”

Luke 10:20

Answer

By faith in the Lamb. Whosoever calls on the name of the Lord and trusts Jesus is saved, and his name is written in the Lamb's book of life, never to be blotted out. And now is the day to do it.
