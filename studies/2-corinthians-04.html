Part one

The light

Verses 1–6

Why Paul keeps going, why some people can't see it, and who switched the light on.

Out in the open

The hidden things of dishonesty

Kept in the dark

Secrets, spin, the story you tell people so they won't know. Craftiness: being clever with the truth.

Manifestation of the truth

Said in the open

No tricks, nothing hidden, “in the sight of God.” A man whose life can stand being looked at.

Look at the reason Paul doesn't quit: “as we have received mercy.” He keeps going because of mercy he was given, not strength he has. Mercy is also what lets a man put his secrets down. God has seen it all already.

Most of us in recovery know this one by heart: secrets keep us sick. Today, pick one thing you have been hiding and tell it to a brother you trust.

Blind, not stupid

The god of this world

Blinds

Satan, who runs the age that stands against God. He doesn't have to make a man hate the gospel. He only has to keep his eyes shut to it.

The light of the gospel

Shines

The good news about Christ, who is the image of God: what God looks like when you can finally see Him.

If you remember being blind for years (the drinking, the lies, the excuses), you know what Paul means. Nobody argued you into the light. It came on.

That is hope for every man still stumbling around in the dark: your brother, your dad, the guy you used with. Blindness is not the end. Pray for him by name.

The same voice, a second time

In the beginningGod spoke over a dark, empty deep: “Let there be light” (Genesis 1:3).
→
In your heartHe speaks the same word, and light comes on.

Paul's point: the light was not his idea or his doing. We preach not ourselves. A man who knows God switched the light on in him doesn't need to be impressive, and he can't take credit.

And where do you see it? “In the face of Jesus Christ.” Read the gospels slowly and look at His face. That is what God is like.

Part two

Treasure in clay

Verses 7–12

The best news in the chapter, for anyone who feels cracked.

Cracks let the light out

Earthen vessels were cheap clay pots, used for water, oil, and trash. Nobody keeps treasure in one. That's Paul's point.

God put the treasure, the light of the gospel, in men who are ordinary and breakable, so everybody can see “the power may be of God, and not of us.” A perfect jar would get the credit. A cracked one can't.

Your past, your weakness, your record: God is not embarrassed by the clay. What He asks is that you don't hide the cracks and call it a perfect jar.

Four hits, four “not”s

  1. Troubled on every sidesqueezed from all directionsyet not distressednever boxed in with no room left
  2. Perplexedno answers, at a lossbut not in despairat a loss, but not utterly lost
  3. Persecutedhunted and chasedbut not forsakenchased, but never left behind by God
  4. Cast downknocked flat on the groundbut not destroyedknocked down, not knocked out

Notice that every line is honest about the hit. Paul does not say the pressure is fake. He says it is not the whole story.

Knocked down, not knocked out.

“For a just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again: but the wicked shall fall into mischief.”

Proverbs 24:16

If you went down yesterday (a drink, a drug, a blowup, a lie), you are on the ground, and that is real. Own it, plainly, to God and to someone you trust. Then read the next word: “riseth up again.” God's mercy is not used up by one more fall.

Get up today. Not tomorrow, today. Wash your face, call your brother, go to work. Cast down is not destroyed.

Wearing out for somebody else

Paul's bodybeaten, tired, always in danger: “the dying of the Lord Jesus.”
→
Your lifeJesus' life shows through, and you get the good of it.

Paul's hard life isn't wasted. Every scar is a place where Jesus' life shows. “Death worketh in us, but life in you.” What it cost him, it gave them.

That works for you too. The hard years you came through are the reason a younger man will listen when you say, I've been there, and Jesus got me out.

Part three

Not losing heart

Verses 13–18

How a man keeps going when the days are long: by looking at the right thing.

Two “therefore”s

Because we believe“I believed, and therefore have I spoken” (a line from Psalm 116:10)
→
thereforewe speak up about it.
Because God raised Jesusthe grave has already lost once.
→
thereforeHe will raise us too, and “present us with you.”

Faith that never says anything isn't doing much. And the hope under it all is a body raised, a reunion, and a Savior who did it first. Whatever today throws at you, it is not the last word.

Two men in one skin

The outward man is the body: tired, aging, maybe wrecked by years of abuse. It is wearing down, and no pill stops that.

The inward man is who you really are, and God is renewing him day by day. Not all at once, but one day at a time. Sound familiar?

Don't judge your progress by the mirror. Some days the mirror looks worse and the inside is stronger than it has ever been.

Weighing it out

Paul had been flogged, stoned, shipwrecked, and jailed (2 Corinthians 11:23–27). He did not call that light because it didn't hurt. He called it light because he put it on a scale next to glory.

One side: trouble, for a moment. The other: glory with real weight, and it lasts for ever. The Hebrew word for glory means weight. It is not close.

God doesn't tell you the pain is nothing. He says it is going somewhere, and it will be outweighed.

Look at what you can't see

Temporal means it lasts only for a time. A man who stares only at what he can see (the bills, the cravings, the people who won't forgive him) loses heart.

So Paul says look at what he cannot yet see: God, a promise, a resurrection, a home. Today, when the day gets heavy, stop and say out loud what is true and unseen: I am His, and this is not the end.

Points to remember

  1. The light“God, who commanded the light to shine out of darkness, hath shined in our hearts” 4:6The light isn't ours. The God who said “Let there be light” switched it on in us.
  2. Clay jars“We have this treasure in earthen vessels” 4:7God uses ordinary, cracked people, so the credit goes to Him.
  3. Down, not out“Cast down, but not destroyed” 4:9A hard blow is not the end. Get up again today.
  4. Inside“The inward man is renewed day by day” 4:16The body wears out, but God is renewing you from the inside.
  5. A moment“Our light affliction, which is but for a moment” 4:17Weigh the trouble against the glory and it's outweighed.
  6. Not quitting“As we have received mercy, we faint not” 4:1Mercy received, not strength, is what keeps a man going.

Question 1 of 6

The light

Where does the light in a believer's heart come from?

What verses tell us about this?

“Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.”

John 8:12

Answer

From God. The One who said “Let there be light” over the dark deep has shone into our hearts, and what He shows us is the face of Jesus Christ. We didn't produce it, and we can't take credit for it.

Question 2 of 6

Clay jars

Why does God put His treasure in cracked clay jars?

What verses tell us about this?

“And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in my infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me.”

2 Corinthians 12:9

Answer

So that the power is plainly God's and not ours. A perfect jar would take the credit; a cracked one can't. God isn't embarrassed by weakness, and He doesn't wait for us to be flawless before He uses us.

Question 3 of 6

Down, not out

What does Paul say about being knocked down?

What verses tell us about this?

“For a just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again: but the wicked shall fall into mischief.”

Proverbs 24:16

Answer

That it hurts, and that it is not the end. Cast down, but not destroyed. A man can fall and still rise again, because God hasn't left him. So get up today.

Question 4 of 6

Inside

What is happening on the inside while the outside wears out?

What verses tell us about this?

“And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.”

Romans 12:2

Answer

God is renewing the inside man, day by day. The body ages, but the man God is making grows stronger. One day at a time is how He does it.

Question 5 of 6

A moment

Why does Paul call his troubles light?

What verses tell us about this?

“For I reckon that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory which shall be revealed in us.”

Romans 8:18

Answer

Because he weighs them against glory. The trouble lasts for a moment, and the glory is heavy and lasts for ever. Paul doesn't say the pain isn't real. He says it is outweighed.

Question 6 of 6

Not quitting

Why does Paul not quit?

What verses tell us about this?

Answer

Because of mercy received, a treasure worth carrying, and a hope that can't be seen yet. He keeps looking at the things that last. The outside wears down while the inside is renewed, so he doesn't faint.
